Title: Haiku 5 is available
Date: 2026-09-04

Haiku 5 (`claude-haiku-5`) is available on the API and in Claude Code. It has a
1M token context window and a training cutoff of March 2026. Haiku 4.5 will be
retired no earlier than 15 October 2026; move workloads to Haiku 5.

The `effort` frontmatter field now accepts `minimal` in addition to `low`,
`medium`, `high`, `xhigh` and `max`.
